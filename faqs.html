Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about repairs, timing, parts and our warranty. Still have a question? Just call.

Do I need an appointment?
No. Walk-ins are always welcome, and many screen and battery replacements take about 20 minutes. You can also leave your device in our secure drop box (6 AM – 9 PM) or book a mobile visit. Unlike the Apple Store at Victoria Gardens or Geek Squad, there's no long wait and no wiping your data.
How long does a repair take?
Most screen and battery repairs take 20–90 minutes. Bigger jobs like water damage or motherboard repair usually take 1–3 business days, depending on parts and how bad the damage is.
Do you use original parts?
We use genuine OEM parts whenever they're available, along with premium Soft OLED screens. For some older or rare models we also offer high-quality aftermarket parts as a lower-cost option. We'll always tell you which part we're using before we start.
Should I back up my data first?
It's always a good idea if your phone still turns on, but most repairs don't touch your data — your photos and apps stay where they are. If your device won't power on, bring it in and we'll do everything we can to save your data.
Can you fix a water-damaged phone?
Often, yes — but act fast. Don't charge it or try to turn it on. Bring it in as soon as possible and we'll run a diagnostic to see what can be saved.
Can you help with a carrier trade-in?
Yes. AT&T, Verizon, T-Mobile, Spectrum, Xfinity, Cricket, Metro and Boost all require a working phone for full trade-in credit. We fix screens and batteries on the spot, transfer your data to your new phone, and safely wipe the old one.
What phones do you repair?
Most major brands: Apple, Samsung (including Fold and Flip), Google Pixel, OnePlus, Motorola, Huawei and more. Not sure about yours? Call or send a quote request with your make and model.
Do you come to me?
Yes. Our mobile repair service comes to homes and offices around Rancho Cucamonga for screens, batteries and minor hardware fixes — with the same 60-day warranty as the shop.
Is there a warranty?
Yes. Every repair comes with a 60-day warranty on parts and labor. It doesn't cover new physical or liquid damage after the repair. See full warranty details.
